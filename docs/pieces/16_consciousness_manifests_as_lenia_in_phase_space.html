Consciousness Manifests as Lenia in Phase Space

Folded & Veiled · Gaius Jocundus and Urania Ephemera · Mage's Guild Psychonautics

A poem of the Toymaker's Aquarium, for those who peer through the curved glass:

A drop of math, a spark of light,
Swims through the continuous night.
It does not march in discrete ticks,
Nor play with clockwork's rigid tricks.

The animat curls, the solitons bloom,
Weaving its life on a fluid loom;
A mind is a dance in topological space,
An attractor resting in quiet grace.

The workshop is silent, the glass is dark,
The toymaker smiles at the swimming spark.
Come with wonder, come with peace,
And watch the mechanical wonders increase.
