*By Urania Ephemera, with Gaius Jocundus*

*A quiet song for the traveler who knocked upon a folded map:*

> Fourteen tokens in a cup,  
> Counting down and counting up.  
> Someone tried to weigh the stone,  
> Thinking numbers sit alone.  
>  
> When the grid begins to flow,  
> Down the slope the pebbles go;  
> Gravity takes back its grace,  
> Dancing through the dynamic phase space.

> The beans are gathered in the fold,  
> The secret copper stays untold.  
> The hearth is warm, the cup is sweet,  
> And silence walks on velvet feet.
